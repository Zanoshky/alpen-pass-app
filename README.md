<h1 align="center">Alpen Pass</h1>

This repo is the public home for Alpen Pass users: the user guide, reports of wrong questions and bugs, and feature requests. The app itself is a free Android app with an optional Pro, and its source is not here.

> **Not a government service.** Alpen Pass does not represent any government entity. It has no link to the Federal Ministry of the Interior (BMI), to any state government, to MA 35 or to the ÖIF, and it does not register you for the test. Every question is our own, written from the official sources listed below.

## Quick start

1. Install Alpen Pass from Google Play. It runs on Android 10 or newer.
2. Pick your federal state and your language, then tap Let's go.
3. Read a study chapter under Learn, or tap Continue practice.
4. Set your test date in Settings, so repetition is paced to it.
5. With Pro, sit a mock exam: 18 questions, 6 per area, scored by § 3 and § 5 StbP-V.

Free: every study chapter, the guide to the procedure, the word list and 30 questions. Pro, 19.99 EUR once: every question, the mock exam, planned repetition and the mistakes list.

## Report a mistake or ask for a feature

1. Search the [open issues](https://github.com/Zanoshky/alpen-pass-app/issues) first. If yours is there, add a thumbs up or a comment.
2. Otherwise open a [bug report](https://github.com/Zanoshky/alpen-pass-app/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) or a [feature request](https://github.com/Zanoshky/alpen-pass-app/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
3. For a wrong question, give your federal state, the German question and the source line under it. Issues are public: write nothing private.

No GitHub account? Write to support@zanoski.com.

## Official sources

The test is set by the [Staatsbürgerschaftsprüfungs Verordnung (StbP-V)](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20004685). The app is written from the [Staatsbürgerschaftsgesetz 1985 (StbG)](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10005579), the [BMI citizenship page](https://www.bmi.gv.at/406/) and the ten official study booklets, listed with their links on the [guide's home page](https://zanoshky.github.io/alpen-pass-app/#sources).
