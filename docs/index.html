Alpen Pass

Practice for the Austrian citizenship test on Android. The test has 18 questions: 6 on democracy, 6 on the history of Austria and 6 on the history of your federal state, each with four answers of which one to three are right. Alpen Pass trains exactly that, for all 9 states, with over 1000 questions that each name the official booklet and page they come from.

Get it on Google Play Read the guide
Not a government service

Alpen Pass does not represent any government entity. It has no link to the Federal Ministry of the Interior (BMI), to any state government, to MA 35 or to the ÖIF, and it does not register you for the test. It issues no certificate and gives no guarantee of passing. Every question and study text is our own wording, written from the official sources below; the questions are practice questions, not the questions of the real test.

Quick start

  1. Install Alpen Pass from Google Play. It runs on Android 10 or newer.
  2. Pick your federal state and your language on the welcome screen, then tap Let's go.
  3. Read a study chapter under Learn, or tap Continue practice to answer questions.
  4. Set your test date in Settings, so repetition is paced to it.
  5. With Pro, sit a mock exam under Exam: 18 questions, scored like the real test.

What is in the app

  • Over 1000 practice questions: 191 on democracy, 178 on the history of Austria, and 61 to 90 for each of the 9 federal states.
  • Study chapters in our own words for every chapter of the official booklets, with the pages and a key to the official PDF.
  • Path to citizenship: ten short chapters on deadlines, conditions, German and the test, income, the procedure, documents and fees.
  • Words: 186 hard terms as flip cards, the German word in front and a simple explanation on the back.
  • A mock exam scored by § 3 and § 5 of the exam regulation (StbP-V), with an optional 2 hour clock.
  • The whole app in 10 languages: German, Bosnian, Croatian, Serbian, Turkish, Arabic, Dari, Albanian, Russian and English. Questions stay in German, as in the test, with a help line in your language.
  • Offline, no account, no ads, and no internet permission.

Free and Pro

Free: every study chapter, the guide to the procedure, the word cards and 30 practice questions, 10 per area. Pro is one optional purchase of 19.99 EUR, the same price in every country, paid once: every question, the mock exam, planned repetition and the mistakes list.
